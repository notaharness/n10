"Works with" and "Runs on". They name what n10 runs alongside; they don't
claim an endorsement or partnership. Every mark is a trademark of its
owner, whatever the licence on the drawing.

Simple Icons has removed OpenAI's and Microsoft's marks at their owners'
request, which is why those two come from Wikimedia Commons. Apple and
Microsoft license their logos for third-party use only case by case; the
site shows them, as download pages commonly do, to say which operating
systems n10 runs on.

Every mark is drawn in the page's text colour, the one-colour form the
guidelines above allow, and is not recoloured, stretched or animated.
In `landing/works-with.tsx` each mark takes up under half its tile, and
only the faint panes behind it and the graph above it move.
